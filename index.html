      What is PAS 128?

      PAS 128 is the UK specification for underground utility detection, verification and location. It grades survey results by quality level, so you know exactly how much confidence you can place in the data before you dig.

      QL-DDesktop records search

      Existing records from asset owners are gathered and combined. This is broadly what your LSBUD search provides.

      QL-CSite reconnaissance

      Visible evidence on site, such as covers, markers and street furniture, is recorded and checked against the records.

      QL-BDetection

      Services are traced and mapped using electromagnetic location and ground penetrating radar. Most pre-excavation surveys target this level.

      QL-AVerification

      Services are physically exposed, for example by vacuum excavation, to confirm exact position and depth.

      What a utility strike really costs

      A strike is rarely just a repair bill. The knock-on costs usually far exceed the cost of surveying first.

      ⏱

      Project delays

      Work stops while the damage is made safe and repaired, crews stand idle and your programme slips.

      ⚡

      Service outages

      Homes and businesses can lose gas, power, water or connectivity, with the asset owner recovering its costs from you.

      Repair costs

      Emergency repair, reinstatement, asset owner recharges and potential claims, plus higher insurance premiums.

      Safety implications

      Gas escapes and electrical strikes can cause serious injury. HSE guidance (HSG47) expects services to be located before you dig.
